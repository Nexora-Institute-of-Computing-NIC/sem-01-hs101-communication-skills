# HS101 — Communication Skills — PLAN

**Semester:** 1 · **Credits:** 3 · **Level:** Beginner
**Theory:** 30 hrs · **Practical:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Word processor, presentation software

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Foundations of Communication (6 lectures)
1. What is communication — model and elements
2. Verbal vs non-verbal communication
3. Barriers to effective communication
4. Active listening
5. Formal vs informal communication
6. Communication in the workplace

### Unit 2 — Written Communication (8 lectures)
1. Grammar essentials — parts of speech
2. Sentence structure and common errors
3. Paragraphs and coherence
4. Email etiquette
5. Formal letters and applications
6. Reports — structure and style
7. Technical writing — clarity, precision, concision
8. Plagiarism and citation basics

### Unit 3 — Spoken Communication (8 lectures)
1. Pronunciation and clarity
2. Public speaking — fear and preparation
3. Structuring a talk — intro, body, conclusion
4. Visual aids — slides that help, not hurt
5. Group discussions
6. Presentations — voice, posture, eye contact
7. Q&A handling
8. Debates and persuasive speaking

### Unit 4 — Reading and Listening (4 lectures)
1. Skimming, scanning, and close reading
2. Reading technical documents efficiently
3. Listening for information vs critical listening
4. Note-taking systems

### Unit 5 — Professional Communication (4 lectures)
1. Interview basics
2. Behavioural interviews — STAR method
3. Negotiation and assertiveness
4. Cross-cultural communication

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Self-introduction recording and self-critique
2. Email etiquette — inbox review exercise
3. Formal letter writing
4. Report writing on a technical topic
5. Technical documentation rewrite
6. Group discussion — a technology debate
7. One-minute impromptu talk
8. Structured presentation — 5 minutes
9. Slide design exercise — critique a bad deck
10. Q&A simulation
11. Listening exercise — summarise a podcast
12. Skim-and-summarise — a technical paper
13. Mock interview round 1 — behavioural
14. Mock interview round 2 — technical
15. Cross-cultural case study

## Assignments

1. Email portfolio — three professional emails
2. Formal letter — an application
3. Technical report — one topic
4. Presentation — record and submit
5. Mock interview reflection
